Design proposal · Live campaign auction web app · v1.0 · 25 Oct 2026

Auction UI/UX: three design directions

Three visual and interaction directions for the campaign-based live auction (Vietnamese-first, VND default, 50–100 concurrent bidders, mostly on phones). Each direction comes with a palette, typography, a component set and rendered mockups. Interaction, accessibility, responsive and edge-state rules are shared across all three, and the document ends with a comparison and a recommendation.

1. Brief & design principles

A campaign is a short, scheduled event: products go live one after another in the order the admin set, each with a hard end time that is pushed back when someone bids in the final window. The UI therefore has to work like a live event (urgency, shared moments, instant feedback) and like a store (trust, legible prices, clear rules) at the same time. Every direction follows the same five principles:

1. The price is the hero. Current price, minimum next bid and time left are always visible together. On a phone they stay above the fold, and the bid control is sticky.
2. Never lie about state. A bid is "Đang gửi…" until the server acknowledges it. "Leading" only appears after the ack. While reconnecting, prices are shown as stale and bidding is disabled.
3. One tap to recover. Outbid, same-price conflicts and below-minimum errors all offer a single button pre-filled with the new minimum.
4. Celebrate fairly. The winners board ranks by price gain (final − start), so a cheap item that drew a bidding war can top the board.
5. Vietnamese first. Copy, number format (1.200.000 ₫) and diacritic-safe fonts are the default. English is a complete second locale.

Sample data used throughout: campaign “Đấu giá Mùa Thu 2026 – Gây quỹ Trăng Rằm”; step prices of 100.000–500.000 ₫; bidder names masked as first two characters + *** + last character (e.g. Nguyễn → Ng***n).

2. Direction A: Arena Live

Dark broadcastSports-score energyMono numerals

Concept. The campaign is presented as a live broadcast. A dark stage puts one featured lot under a "spotlight", prices are set in a monospace scoreboard face that ticks like a stadium display, and an acid-lime price colour and hot-red urgency colour do the emotional work. A bottom ticker streams masked bids ("Tr***g vừa trả 24.500.000 ₫") so people feel the room even when they are only watching. Extensions get a cyan "+30 s" burst, the moment the audience notices.

Target vibe: electric, competitive, "everyone is watching". Pick it when the campaign is the event: company year-end parties, charity galas projected on a big screen, gaming or tech audiences, evening sessions where dark UI is comfortable.

3. Direction B: Chợ Phiên (clean marketplace)

Light e-commerceFamiliar like Shopee / Tikishadcn-native

Concept. "Chợ phiên" is the Vietnamese periodic market: a familiar, trustworthy place that comes alive on a schedule. The UI borrows the conventions Vietnamese shoppers already know from large e-commerce apps: white cards on a cool grey canvas, a confident blue for actions, orange for prices, and tabs for Đang diễn ra / Sắp diễn ra / Đã kết thúc. Excitement comes from state rather than decoration: a timer chip that turns amber and then red, an orange flash on price change, and a violet extension badge. Because it stays close to shadcn/ui defaults, it is the cheapest direction to build and the easiest to re-skin for a client brand.

Target vibe: trustworthy, fast, legible in daylight on a phone. Pick it when the audience is broad (staff, customers, older bidders), people bid from phones during the working day, or the product must carry a client's brand colour later.

4. Direction C: Atelier (premium editorial gallery)

Editorial / auction houseSerif + hairlinesCalm urgency

Concept. Modelled on a printed auction-house catalogue. Every product is a numbered Lot with generous imagery, a serif headline and a short provenance-style description on warm ivory paper. Urgency is conveyed with restraint: the countdown is set in small caps and only turns oxblood in the final 30 seconds, and the extension is announced as a quiet gold rule that reads "Gia hạn · +30 giây". The winners board becomes a "Sổ vàng" (golden ledger) with hairline rules and old-style figures.

Target vibe: prestige, trust, collectibility. Pick it when lots are art, craft, antiques or luxury goods, the audience is older or VIP, and the brand matters more than raw bidding speed (gala dinners, art-for-charity, heritage brands).

5. Interaction & micro-interaction spec

6. Notification & sound permission UX

  1. Never ask on page load. Browsers penalise this, and a cold prompt is usually denied.
  2. Soft-ask at the moment of value: immediately after the user's first successful bid, show an inline card under the bid form: "Nhận thông báo khi có người trả cao hơn?" with the buttons Bật thông báo and Để sau.
  3. The Bật thông báo click is the user gesture. In the same handler, call Notification.requestPermission() and unlock audio (AudioContext.resume() plus playing the outbid sound once at low volume as confirmation).
  4. Granted: show "Đã bật" and a bell icon in the header. Denied: show a short how-to ("Mở cài đặt trang › Thông báo › Cho phép") and fall back to in-tab toast, sound and title flashing ((1) Bị vượt giá · …).
  5. iOS Safari only supports web push for home-screen PWAs. Show "Thêm vào màn hình chính để nhận thông báo" there and rely on sound and toast otherwise.
  6. Header controls: a bell toggle (notifications) and a speaker toggle (sound), both persisted per user.

7. Accessibility

Contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA). Checked pairs: A lime #C6FF3D on #0B0D17 ≈ 16:1; white on CTA #E11D48 ≈ 4.7:1; muted #8F98BD on #0B0D17 ≈ 6.6:1. B price #C2410C on white ≈ 5.2:1; primary #1D4ED8 ≈ 6.7:1. C oxblood #7A1F2B on ivory ≈ 9:1; decorative gold #B08D57 is used only for rules and icons, with #7D5F2A for gold text (≈ 5:1).
Focus & targets. Visible 3 px focus ring (:focus-visible) in each theme's focus colour with a 2 px offset. Minimum target size is 44 × 44 px on touch, and quick-step chips are 48 px high on phones. Enter in the price input submits, and Esc dismisses a toast.
Live regions. One visually hidden aria-live="polite" region per lot announces price changes, throttled to at most one announcement every 5 s with the latest value ("Giá mới 25.000.000 đồng, Ph***h"). Outbid and errors use role="alert". The countdown is not live; it announces only at 60 s, 30 s and 10 s and on extension ("Gia hạn thêm 30 giây").
Reduced motion. prefers-reduced-motion: reduce disables pulse, roll, pop, slide and confetti, and keeps colour plus text changes. Sound is independent of motion and controlled by the speaker toggle.
Semantics. Status is never conveyed by colour alone (icon and text). Masked names get an aria-label ("Người đấu giá ẩn danh Ng…n") so screen readers do not read out the asterisks. Prices carry a full accessible name even when abbreviated ("25,5TR" → "25.500.000 đồng").
Language. <html lang="vi"> or "en" switches with the locale so screen readers use the right voice. Mixed snippets get lang on the element.

9. Language & currency

Language switcher. A segmented "VI | EN" control in the header (desktop) or account sheet (phone). The default comes from the profile, then localStorage, then navigator.language, then vi. Switching changes UI copy only, never product content (admin may optionally enter an EN name and description; if absent, show the VI text with a small "VI" tag). Copy is about 30% longer in EN, so buttons must not have a fixed width.
Currency. Currency is a campaign setting (VND or USD) chosen by the admin, not an FX conversion, so all bids for a lot are in one currency. Format with Intl.NumberFormat: vi-VN VND gives 1.200.000 ₫ (no decimals); en-US USD gives $1,250 (decimals only if the step is fractional). EN locale with VND shows ₫1,200,000. The "₫ | $" toggle in mockups shows this campaign setting; a display-only approximate conversion could later be shown as muted text "≈ $980".
Input. The price input accepts digits only, formats with separators as you type, and supports the short forms "25tr" / "25m" → 25.000.000. It validates ≥ current + step inline before submission.

11. My bids, login & admin (brief)

My bids. Tabs Tất cả / Dẫn đầu / Bị vượt / Đã thắng (see the Direction B phone mockup). Outbid rows sort to the top while the lot is live and carry an inline "Đặt lại [min]" button. Won rows show next steps (contact, payment).
Login. A single centred card with username, password, "Hiện mật khẩu" toggle and a VI/EN switch; the error "Sai tên đăng nhập hoặc mật khẩu" does not say which field is wrong. A reserved slot below the form ("Đăng nhập bằng SSO công ty") is hidden until SSO ships. After login the user returns to the lot they came from.
Admin. Uses shadcn defaults in every direction (no theming). Products: data table plus a sheet form (images drag-to-reorder, prices with VND formatting, start/end, extend window, extend-by). Campaign: drag-to-reorder list with auto-computed start times and conflict warnings. Live monitor: per-lot cards with current price, bids/min, connected users, last extension, and a "Tạm dừng lot" action behind a confirmation.

Recommendation: build Direction B (Chợ Phiên) as the product foundation, and add Direction A's "live layer" for urgent moments.

B is the fastest to ship on Tailwind + shadcn/ui, the most legible for phone-first bidders in daylight, and the easiest to re-brand per campaign. Its weakness is energy, so borrow the parts of A that carry the event: the lime/red price-flash and countdown-heat behaviour (mapped to B's orange and red tokens), the "+30 GIÂY" pop badge, the masked bid ticker on the home page, and an optional dark stage mode for projecting the live lot at the venue. Keep C as a theme preset for future art or gala campaigns. Because all three share the same component set and token names (--pri, --pr, --hot, --ext…), C becomes a token file plus a serif font rather than a second UI.
